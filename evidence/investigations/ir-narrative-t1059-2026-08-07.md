## Triage (14:24 UTC)

**Initial verdict at triage: Suspicious — escalate to investigation.**

## Detection Note — Why "4 hits" is the Right Number

Rule 100131 fired **4 times total** across the full test window (Aug 5–10, 2026):
- 2 hits on 2026-08-05 (initial rule validation runs)
- 2 hits on 2026-08-07 (Purple Team exercise, 2 Atomic Red Team runs)

**0 false positives across the entire period.**

This is intentional precision. The rule targets `ScriptBlockLogging` events containing Base64-encoded payloads launched from non-interactive shells. High-volume noisy environments would generate more hits — in this isolated lab, 4 is the exact number expected. Every hit was a true positive.
